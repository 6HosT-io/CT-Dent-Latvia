Technology

What is cone-beam CT (CBCT)?

The Hyperion X9™ is a non-invasive, fast and precise X-ray examination delivering high-quality 3D diagnostic images at a lower radiation dose than conventional CT.

  • Precise bone measurement
  • Mandibular nerve canal visualisation
  • Maxillary sinus assessment
  • Root fracture detection
  • Localisation of impacted teeth
  • Clarification of oral pathology

Questions

Frequently asked

Is a doctor’s referral required?+
Yes — examinations are performed with a doctor’s referral. The form can be filled in online or downloaded as PDF.
How long does the examination take?+
The scan itself takes only a few minutes; the whole visit about 15–20 minutes.
When will I get the results?+
Results are usually available the same day; the referring doctor receives them in the online portal.
Is the scan safe?+
Yes — cone-beam technology uses a significantly lower radiation dose than conventional CT.

CT-Dent · utility bar · round 3

From E — three ways to zero overlap

You loved E’s corner capsule but not even its minimal overlap with content. E1, E2 and E3 keep everything E makes great — pure header, glass capsule, pocket info card — while the capsule lives only in reserved space: the screen margin (E1), a reserved bottom band (E2), or its own lane that docks into the header (E3). All demos are interactive: open the (i) cards, flip the theme, scroll E3. Previous rounds remain below for reference.

theme
E1

Edge rail — E, docked to the screen margin

The capsule becomes a slim vertical rail pinned to the right edge of the screen, vertically centered in the page margin — it never enters the content column, so overlap is zero on desktop. Tap-to-call phone, LV/EN/RU stacked, a vertical theme slider, and the (i) card slides out to the left. Below ~1100px the rail auto-hides and the phone chip moves into the header. Open the (i) in the demo.

  • +zero overlap — lives entirely in the margin
  • +signature luxury/editorial detail
  • +always at thumb distance, every scroll position
  • −phone is icon-only (number lives in header & footer on small screens)
  • −needs a header fallback on mobile

E2

Reserved dock band — mathematically zero overlap

A slim dark status bar pinned to the bottom of the viewport — the page reserves its 50px, so it can never cover anything. Phone, address and hours always visible on the left; language, theme and the (i) card on the right. Mission-control feel for a medical-tech brand.

  • +zero overlap by construction — the space is reserved
  • +all info permanently visible, no clicks
  • +strong pro-tool character
  • −a permanent bar returns (at the bottom, not above the hero)
  • −costs 50px of viewport height

E3

Docking capsule — floats in its own lane

E’s capsule, but it never floats over content: on arrival it sits in its own reserved row between header and hero; once you scroll, it docks into the header as a compact group (try scrolling). Address & hours stay one click away via the (i) card in both states.

  • +capsule aesthetic with literally zero content overlap
  • +big tappable phone + full controls on arrival
  • +header becomes fully self-contained on scroll
  • −adds a ~60px row above the hero at page top (reclaimed on scroll)
  • −two states = slightly more logic

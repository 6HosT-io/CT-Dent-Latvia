iRYS manual

10 steps with screenshots — from inserting the disc to the 3D view, measurements and implant planning.

This manual walks you through the iRYS software step by step — from inserting the disc to measurements, nerve-canal marking, implant planning and the 3D view.

  • Referring a patient for a CBCT scan reduces the risk of unqualified work.
  • Most doctors worldwide use CBCT before implantation and other oral and jaw surgery.
  • To learn more about iRYS, visit us — book in advance by phone or e-mail.
01
MANUAL

10 steps with screenshots

01

Disc and installation

Insert the CD with the patient data into the computer. If you are using iRYS for the first time, copy the installation file from the disc and install it.

02

Opening the disc

Wait for the disc window ("Auto play") to appear and click it — or open "My computer" and select the CD drive.

03

Selecting the patient

In the violet window, click once on the patient’s first and last name, then press "Open".

04

New multiplanar view

In the window showing the patient data at the top, click "New multiplaner" on the left — the first page of images appears.

05

Slice settings

Moving the mouse up or down over the axial slice images (top left) selects the height ("Axial setup"). Dragging the arc dots on the selected axial slice generates the panoramic image; change its thickness in the "Panorex setup" field — choose 1 to see the mandibular canal clearly, or 20 for the conventional panoramic view. In the "Cross setup" field, choose the thickness and spacing of the coronal slices — e.g. 1, 2 or 3 mm.

06

Storing the settings

To store the settings, click "Store image" on the left side of the window. You can always return and change the settings via "Modify".

07

The main analysis view

Four main objects are visible: axial slices (top left), the panoramic image (top right), coronal slices and the 3D view. Yellow numbered vertical lines on the panorama indicate the position of each coronal slice. With the mouse you can move the lines and choose the slices to analyse; the red line corresponds to the coronal slice in the red frame.

08

Toolbar: measurements, markers, implants

"Distance toolbar" lets you measure coronal slices vertically and horizontally; measurements can be deleted one by one or all at once (right mouse button exits the tool). "Show markers toolbar" marks the mandibular nerve canal. "Show implants toolbar" → green plus "New implant" → "Implant editor": choose the size and manufacturer, then place the implant on a coronal slice or the panorama.

09

3D view

Select the 3D view section and click "New 3D". Here you can change the view position and apply filters such as the cephalometric view and simulated gums. To save the image, click "store image" at the top right.

10

Brightness and contrast

Double-clicking any image opens its brightness and contrast settings.

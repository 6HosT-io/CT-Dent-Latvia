Cookie Policy

Which cookies we use and how to manage your choice.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser so the website works and we can improve the experience.

What we use

Necessary — site operation and remembering your choices (language, theme, consent); always active.

Analytics — anonymised visit statistics, only with consent.

Marketing — event measurement for campaigns, only with consent.

Third-party cookies

No third-party cookies are set on this site without consent; embedded content (map) loads only after your choice.

Managing consent

You can withdraw or change consent at any time via the cookie settings in your browser or by clicking “Cookie settings” in the site footer.

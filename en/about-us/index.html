About CT-Dent

We are an independent dental scanning centre: our only task is quality diagnostics — without competing with treating doctors.

CT-Dent is the only independent dental scanning centre in Latvia, unaffiliated with other medical institutions. Many dentists feel uncomfortable sending patients to competing clinics — with us that problem does not exist: we do not provide treatment and do not compete with your doctor.

We are part of the international CT-Dent network that serves more than 12 000 dental specialists daily and unites over 130 scanning centres worldwide.

At our centre in Riga, Cēsu iela 31, experienced radiology specialists operate the MyRay Hyperion X9 cone-beam CT — a unit delivering high-resolution 3D images at a low radiation dose.

Our values

  • Independence — results serve only the interests of the treating doctor.
  • Precision — standardised protocols and regular quality control.
  • Respect for time — fast booking and result delivery.

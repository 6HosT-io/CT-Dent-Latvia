How does it work?

Four simple steps from referral to results.

I.

Referral

The doctor fills in the referral online or downloads the PDF form.

II.

Booking

We call to agree a convenient time — or accept walk-ins.

III.

Scanning

The visit takes about 15 minutes with the Hyperion X9.

IV.

Results

The doctor receives 3D images in the online portal, usually the same day.
